Project

Guerrera Camaleón

2023
  • Props design
  • Art assistant
Watch trailer ↗

Guerrera Camaleón is the flagship animated series from Trébol Pro Animations S.A.S., a pioneering digital animation studio founded in 2016 and based in Santa Marta, Colombia, specialized in managing and producing audiovisual projects. I joined as prop designer, helping bring the show's objects and environments to life.

Process

Working as part of the art team, I designed props that needed to read clearly across scenes and hold up through the characters' movement and interactions, keeping proportions, color and finish consistent with the series' style guide from episode to episode.

Result

Guerrera Camaleón was nominated for the 2025 Premios Catalina, one of Colombia's top animation awards — a recognition of the show's craft that I was proud to contribute to as part of the prop design team.
